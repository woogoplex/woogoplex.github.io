# Writing guidelines

## Privacy (don't post these)

- Your school's name
- Your neighborhood (city-level is fine — no district, apartment, or street names)
- Photos showing anyone's face (including your own)
- Any academy or tutoring center you attend
- Family members' real names

Why: this site is on the open internet, and anything posted here can stick
around somewhere even after you delete it.

## Attitude toward your own writing

- Don't edit old posts to change your mind — write a new one instead. A
  record of how your thinking changed over time is the whole point of this
  site.
- Fixing typos or broken links is always fine.
- If you use someone else's writing or data, link to the source. A single
  link is enough.
- Consistency beats polish. A short post is better than no post.
